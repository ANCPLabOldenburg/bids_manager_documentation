## The header

{{ figure("header", "The header, with a project open.") }}

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| The logo | Opens **About**: the version, the lab, the authors and the acknowledgements. |
| **Home**, **Converter**, **Editor** | The three views. BIDS Manager opens on Home; with a project open it returns to the Converter or the Editor, whichever you used last. |
| The project switcher | The open project's name. Click it for its folder, the recent projects, and **Open or create another… (Home)**. |
| **Guide** | Switches the guide on or off: in each view it asks what you would like to do, then rings the control to use and says what to do there ([First launch](/start/first-launch/#let-the-guide-show-you)). Off until you switch it on, and remembered. |
| The gear | **Settings** ([Settings](/reference/settings/)). |
| The sun or the moon | The theme menu ([First launch](/start/first-launch/#choose-a-theme)). |

## The views

- **Home** creates a dataset, opens one, and lists the recent projects (select several to remove them from the list or delete them).
- **The Converter** scans raw data and converts it: [The Converter](/reference/interface/converter/).
- **The Editor** validates, edits and views the dataset: [The Editor](/reference/interface/editor/).

## The status bar

At the bottom: what BIDS Manager is doing or did last, the version, and **Check updates**.

## Panels

The Converter and the Editor are made of panels drawn as cards. Most panels can be folded and detached, and every panel can be resized (the Inspection panel and the Editor's viewer cannot be folded, only detached; Properties detaches together with the Inspection panel):

- **folded**: side panels fold to a strip along their edge, with the panel's name down it; top and bottom panels fold from their title bar. Click the strip or the title to unfold.
- **resized**: drag the grip (three dots) between two panels.
- **detached**: the icon at the right of a panel's title opens it in its own window; close the window to put it back.
