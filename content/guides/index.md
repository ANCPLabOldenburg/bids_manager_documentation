## Kinds of data

::: cards 2
- [**MRI** Two participants on a Siemens scanner: subjects and sessions, fieldmaps, physiological logs.](/guides/mri/)
- [**Advanced MRI** Diffusion maps, spectroscopy, and sequences named one thing and acquired as another.](/guides/mri-advanced/)
- [**PET** A DICOM scan with its dose sheet and blood curves, and a scan in the ECAT format.](/guides/pet/)
- [**EEG** Recordings whose file names do not say the task: naming tasks and runs, events, montage.](/guides/eeg/)
- [**MEG** Sessions from dates, empty-room recordings, and what the FIF file already knows.](/guides/meg/)
- [**Eye tracking** EyeLink recordings per eye, paired with the run they were recorded during.](/guides/eye-tracking/)
- [**Several kinds of data at once** What changes when one study has MRI, PET, EEG, MEG and more.](/guides/multimodal/)
:::
